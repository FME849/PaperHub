# Product Vision

## Problem

Researchers and students struggle to keep track of newly published papers
related to topics they care about.

Reading full papers is time-consuming.

## Solution

A web platform that:
- tracks new papers from arXiv
- groups papers by topics
- summarizes abstracts
- helps users quickly discover relevant research

## Target Users

- students
- researchers
- AI engineers

## MVP

- auth
- follow topics
- fetch papers from arXiv
- summarize abstracts
- search/filter papers
- favorite papers
